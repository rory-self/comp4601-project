Energy measurement — measured on the board, NOT from a synthesis report
=======================================================================
Vitis/Vivado do not report energy. HLS reports resources and timing only; even
Vivado's report_power is an *estimate* from a switching model. These figures are
read from the KV260 SOM's on-board INA260 current/power sensor while the board is
actually running the workload.

Sensor:  /sys/class/hwmon/hwmon0/power1_input     (total SOM power, microwatts)
         (hwmon0 name = "ina260_u14")

Method:  run one engine flat out for 12 s, sampling power ~5x/second, and divide
         average power by measured throughput.

  ./demo_host_arm -x <bitstream> -d /tmp -m sw -t 12     # ARM software only
  ./demo_host_arm -x <bitstream> -d /tmp -m hw -t 12     # FPGA kernel only
  cat /sys/class/hwmon/hwmon0/power1_input               # sample during each run

Measured (12 s sustained load each):

  state                 board power     throughput      energy / byte
  -------------------   -----------     -----------     -------------
  idle                    3.192 W            -                -
  ARM software (tANS)     3.349 W        54.3 MB/s        61.6 nJ/B
  FPGA kernel  (tANS)     3.422 W       146.7 MB/s        23.3 nJ/B

  -> FPGA uses 2.64x LESS ENERGY PER BYTE

Note the FPGA draws MORE instantaneous power (+0.073 W) yet uses less energy,
because it finishes 2.70x sooner -- race to idle. Reporting watts alone would have
made the accelerator look worse; energy per byte is the honest metric.

On an incremental basis (subtracting the 3.192 W idle floor, i.e. the marginal cost
of doing the work): 2.89 nJ/B software vs 1.57 nJ/B hardware = 1.84x better.
