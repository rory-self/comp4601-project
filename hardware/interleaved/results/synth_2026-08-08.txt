CITE 273.97 MHz. The board top is what gets linked into the bitstream; the
standalone coder is a block that is never built on its own.

The 293 MHz in older notes was the standalone number from an earlier revision of
the source. That same top synthesises at 342.47 MHz today.

TWO OBSERVATIONS WORTH REPORTING
 1. The AXI wrapper costs ~20% of the clock (342.47 -> 273.97 MHz). The critical
    path in the BUILT design is the memory interface, not the coder.
 2. replication_full reports the SAME 273.97 MHz, also at a 3.650 ns estimated
    path (see ../../replication_full/REPRODUCIBILITY.md step 2). Two structurally
    different coders landing on an identical critical path is strong evidence
    that neither coder is the limiter -- the shared AXI infrastructure is.

CAUTION ON FMAX-SCALED THROUGHPUT
The "31 M/s predicted" figure quoted elsewhere is the cosim cycle count scaled to
Fmax: 20.679 M/s x (Fmax / 200). That gives 28.3, 30.3 or 35.4 M/s depending on
which Fmax is used. Only two numbers here are directly measured:
  20.679 M/s  cosim, kernel-only, at 200 MHz
  11.667 M/s  real fabric
Cite those. Label anything Fmax-scaled as a projection.
