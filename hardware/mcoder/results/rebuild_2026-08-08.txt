THIS RESOLVES TWO OPEN QUESTIONS ABOUT THE PRESENTATION
=============================================================================

1. "65 KB -> 40 KB, ~39% reduction" (slides 2 and 10) is measurement B above:
   65536 -> 40406 B = 38.3% reduction. It is an M-CODER figure, not a
   replication_full figure. replication_full on the same image gives 42661 B.

2. The slide-8 chart's M-coder bar of 21.16 M sym/s is also measurement B
   (22.25 M sym/s here; 21.16 on the slightly slower original build -- the ratio
   21.16/22.25 tracks the 31.12/33.07 kernel-only ratio).

   But the other three bars are measurement A. So the chart mixes timing
   boundaries: three kernel-only figures against one that includes host<->device
   transfer. Measured consistently, the M-coder bar is 33.07 M sym/s, and the
   speedup over the 3.47 M sym/s ARM baseline is 9.5x, not 6.09x.

   The 6.09x is doubly mixed: an image-demo numerator (21.16) over a kernel-only
   denominator (3.470). Using the image demo on both sides gives 9.82x
   (2.26562 -> 22.2465), which is what demo_arm itself prints.

   The slide UNDERSTATES the M-coder. Either boundary is defensible; mixing them
   is not.
